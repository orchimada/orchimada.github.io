02The rules

Two tiers. Look right, then be ours.

Ten rules borrow the school's fidelity. Seven more turn a drawing into a model.

fidelity · 1–10

Make it look right

Decompose then compose. Color is a stable identity, never decoration. Reveal the hidden. The diagram leads, prose only glues. Annotate in place. Quiet chrome, loud content. Spatial order mirrors causality. One house style. Progressive disclosure. Curiosity as the entry point.

model · 11–17

Make it ours

Model, don't draw, then render many views from the data. Couple the controls. Depth is navigable. The camera has intent. Be data-honest with real units. Linked multi-view. A mechanism is a state machine, and the figure proves the logic.
